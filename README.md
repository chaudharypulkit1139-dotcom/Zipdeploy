# Margdarsh — Stream & Career Guidance for Indian Students

Margdarsh is a psychometric and career advisory Android application built using Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. It evaluates cognitive patterns, learning potential, interests, and personality traits of students, weighed alongside family financial planning, relocation preferences, and aspirations to recommend the right Class 11 stream and matching career paths.

## Key Features

- **Phase 01: Student Psychometric Assessment**
  - 30 guided questions across 6 key cognitive and personality dimensions (Thinking Type, Learning Potential, Interests, Personality, Working Style, Aptitude & Reasoning, Skills & Strengths).
  - 5-point Likert scale with proportional tactile indicators.
  - Question navigation with progress tracking and quick overview selector.
  - Comprehensive scoring across 7 trait domains: Analytical Thinking, Spatial & Numeric Reasoning, Verbal Aptitude, Creative Expression, Social & Empathic Insight, Practical Execution, and Enterprising Drive.

- **Phase 02: Parent Perspectives Survey**
  - 8 questions capturing budget allocation, education loan appetite, relocation flexibility (home city, state, national, international), academic calibre observation, and career aspirations.

- **Phase 03: Report & Recommendation Dashboard**
  - Stream recommendation (Science, Commerce, Humanities) with rationale and subject streams.
  - Interactive Trait Profile bar chart.
  - Stream alignment scores comparison.
  - Strengths and Watch-outs analysis for Class 11 subject preparation.
  - Shortlisted career opportunities with financial cost range, industry scope, tags (High Growth, Emerging, Stable, Uncommon), description, and benefits.
  - Direct counselor contact integration (phone dialer and email intents).
  - Report sharing and session reset capabilities.

## Tech Stack

- **Platform**: Android
- **Language**: Kotlin 2.0
- **UI Framework**: Jetpack Compose with Material Design 3 (M3)
- **Architecture**: MVVM with Kotlin Coroutines & StateFlow
- **Theme**: Warm organic palette (Forest Green `#3F4E3F`, Warm Terracotta Clay `#C58C6D`, Paper Background `#FCF9F5`)
