# Privacy Policy for MargDarsh

**Last Updated:** October 22, 2026  
**Effective Date:** October 22, 2026  

MargDarsh ("we", "our", or "us"), operated in partnership with Rankmakers (Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India), is committed to safeguarding the privacy and personal information of our students and their families. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application **MargDarsh - Stream & Career Intelligence Guidance** ("the Application") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), and Google Play Developer Program Policies.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

When you use MargDarsh to take career evaluations, generate diagnostic dossiers, or book counselling sessions, we collect the following categories of information:

#### A. Candidate & Guardian Profile Information
* **Student Details:** Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp / Phone Number, Current Academic Grade / Class (e.g. Class 8, 9, 10, 11, 12), School or Institution Name, and City / State.
* **Parent / Guardian Details:** Parent / Guardian Full Name, Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number.
* **Unique Identification:** An auto-generated Student Reference ID (e.g. `MD-XXX-XXXX`) to identify evaluation submissions without public disclosure.

#### B. Assessment & Psychometric Responses
* Responses to the multi-dimensional student psychometric questions (measuring cognitive traits such as Analytical, Spatial, Verbal, Enterprising, Social, Creative, and Practical abilities).
* Responses to the parental perspective survey (budgetary preferences, location mobility, parental aspirations, and educational context).
* Evaluated cognitive archetype, stream recommendations, and ranked career paths.

#### C. Payment & Transaction Proofs (Optional / Paid Services)
* Bank Reference Numbers or Unified Payments Interface (UPI) Transaction / UTR Numbers provided by you to confirm bookings or unlock comprehensive 65-page dossiers.
* Digital payment receipts or screenshots uploaded via your device's photo picker to verify transactions for manual booking validation with our counselors.

#### D. Device & Technical Telemetry
* Operating system version, app version, and basic connectivity diagnostic data required to ensure reliable report generation and prevent session interruptions.

---

### 2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal information strictly to deliver, improve, and secure career guidance services:
1. **Personalized Career Analysis:** Calculating aptitude fit scores, cognitive trait distributions, recommended academic streams (Science, Commerce, Humanities, Emerging Tech), and customized job matches.
2. **Comprehensive Diagnostic Dossiers:** Generating your personalized 35-page and 65-page PDF career reports.
3. **1-on-1 Counselling Scheduling:** Connecting students with certified career advisors (including Dr. B.B. Panchal) for one-on-one virtual guidance sessions.
4. **Transaction Verification:** Validating fee waivers, scholarship vouchers, and UPI transaction confirmations.
5. **Direct Communication:** Sending evaluation reports, updates, and appointment reminders via WhatsApp or Email upon request.

We do **not** sell, rent, monetize, or trade user data with advertising brokers or commercial data aggregators.

---

### 3. Data Storage, Cloud Infrastructure & Third Parties

To provide seamless cloud report synchronization and verification:
* **Google Cloud & Google Drive Storage:** Payment proofs and generated report logs are securely archived in dedicated Google Drive folders and encrypted Google Cloud Apps Script endpoints managed under strict authentication.
* **Google Identity Services:** If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In, we receive basic public profile information (name and verified email address) solely to pre-fill your student profile and prevent lost assessment progress.
* **WhatsApp Integration:** All WhatsApp communication links are user-initiated; the Application does not read or inspect your private WhatsApp messages.
* **Zero Third-Party Advertising:** MargDarsh contains no third-party banner ads, programmatic tracking scripts, or ad networks.

---

### 4. Children and Minor Privacy (Under 18)

MargDarsh is designed for school students (Classes 8 through 12). Recognizing the critical importance of minor safety:
* The Application is designed with a dual-evaluation structure that directly involves parents and legal guardians.
* We require parental or guardian consent during registration before accessing premium counselling services or processing payments.
* We do not collect location tracking, microphone audio, background telemetry, or contact book access from minors.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Account / Data Deletion Rights

* **Retention Period:** We retain candidate evaluation scores and student dossiers only as long as necessary to support the student's ongoing educational counselling or as required by regulatory compliance.
* **Right to Access & Rectify:** Parents and students may review or update their registered profile information at any time within the Application.
* **Right to Erasure (Data Deletion):** You have the complete right to request the permanent deletion of all your stored student dossiers, survey answers, contact information, and payment records.
  * **How to Request Deletion:** Send an email to **rankmakersindore@gmail.com** or WhatsApp us at **+91 79098 06620** with the subject line *"Data Deletion Request"* and your registered Phone Number or Student Reference ID.
  * All associated records across our databases and Drive archives will be permanently expunged within 7 business days.

---

### 6. Data Security Practices

* All network transmissions between the Application and our cloud endpoints use industry-standard HTTPS / TLS encryption.
* Device storage utilizes encrypted private SharedPreferences protected by Android system sandboxing.
* Sensitive bank credentials, UPI PINs, and card numbers are never requested, stored, or processed by MargDarsh; payments are conducted externally via certified UPI apps.

---

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect operational, legal, or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Application signifies acceptance of the revised policy.
