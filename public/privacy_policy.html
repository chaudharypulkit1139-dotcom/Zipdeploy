Privacy Policy for MargDarsh

Application: MargDarsh - Career Intelligence & Guidance | Effective Date: October 22, 2026

MargDarsh ("we", "our", or "us"), in operational partnership with Rankmakers (Indore, Madhya Pradesh, India), values the trust you place in us when sharing personal information. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding information collection, usage, and data rights for students, parents, and guardians using our mobile and web applications.

1. Information We Collect

A. Student & Guardian Profiles

  • Student Credentials: Full Name, Email Address, WhatsApp / Phone Number, Grade/Class (Classes 8 through 12), School Name, and City.
  • Parent / Guardian Details: Full Name, Contact Phone / WhatsApp Number.
  • Unique Identifier: Auto-generated Student Reference ID (e.g., MD-XXX-XXXX) to maintain anonymous evaluation records.

B. Psychometric & Assessment Responses

  • Responses to multi-dimensional aptitude questions measuring Analytical, Spatial, Verbal, Enterprising, Social, Creative, and Practical cognitive traits.
  • Parental perspective choices including higher education budget tiers, relocation flexibility, and academic priorities.
  • Evaluated cognitive archetype, stream recommendations, and ranked career fit options.

C. Payment Verifications (Optional Services)

  • Bank UPI Transaction / UTR numbers entered to verify one-on-one career counselling bookings or detailed 65-page audit dossier unlocks.
  • Payment proof screenshots selected via the native photo picker to assist our counselors with manual appointment confirmation.

2. Purpose and Use of Data

Your information is utilized solely for legitimate career guidance and counselling purposes:

  • Generating personalized 35-page and 65-page diagnostic career evaluation dossiers.
  • Scheduling and managing live 1-on-1 virtual sessions with Dr. B.B. Panchal and our counselling team.
  • Verifying scholarship vouchers, fee concessions, and payment statuses.
  • Delivering requested reports via WhatsApp or Email.

We never sell, rent, commercialize, or share student records with third-party advertising companies or marketing agencies.

3. Cloud Infrastructure & Security

  • Cloud Synchronization: Data is synchronized over TLS 1.3 encrypted connections to secure Google Cloud / Google Drive repositories.
  • Google Identity Services: Used optionally to allow students to log in and preserve their assessment progress safely across devices.
  • Storage Safeguards: Device storage utilizes Android sandboxed private storage. Banking PINs, passwords, or credit card numbers are never handled or collected.

4. Children & Minor Protection

MargDarsh is designed for school students. The assessment framework requires active parental or guardian awareness and consent. We do not engage in behavioral profiling, location tracking, or third-party targeted advertising.

5. User Data Deletion Rights

Under applicable Indian and international privacy frameworks, you have the right to request access to or permanent deletion of your data.

To request permanent deletion of your student dossiers, test results, and contact information, send an email to rankmakersindore@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 79098 06620 with your registered phone number or Student Reference ID. All associated cloud records will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.
